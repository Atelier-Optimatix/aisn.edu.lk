About Us

As one of the esteemed Adventist-owned schools in Sri Lanka, we have been preparing students for success in Edexcel London and Sri Lankan Ordinary Level and Advanced Level examinations. Our commitment to academic excellence is reflected in our accreditation by the Adventist Accreditation Association.

Diversity is at the heart of our school community. Students from various ethnic backgrounds, including Sinhala, Tamil, Muslim, and Burgher communities, come together at our school. Here, they study, play, and pray as one united family, fostering a sense of peace, harmony, and mutual respect.

OUR VISION
Inspiring Futures: A Vision of Excellence in Adventist Education
At Adventist International School Negombo Branch, Sri Lanka, our vision is to exceed parental expectations and deliver an exceptional Adventist education within a nurturing and positive school culture.
OUR MISSION
Guiding Hearts: Our Mission for Excellence and Faith
At Adventist International School Negombo Branch, Sri Lanka, our mission is to provide a transformative educational experience that goes beyond academic challenges. We are dedicated to instilling a lifelong love for learning, while introducing students to the Christian way of life and presenting Jesus Christ as a personal Friend and Savior. Our ultimate aim is to equip students to become not only diligent and productive citizens of this world but also prepared for the eternal life to come.
OUR OBJECTIVE
Cultivating Character: Objectives for Holistic Education
  • Building Strong Foundations: Empowering Students with Academic Skills and Lifelong Curiosity
  • Character in Action: Cultivating Christ-like Virtues in Students
  • Nurturing Love and Respect: Fostering a Sense of Belonging to School, Home, Humanity, and Country
  • Embracing Diversity, Inspiring Excellence: Developing Tolerance, Courtesy, Efficiency, and Leadership
  • Empowering Future Leaders: Equipping Students to Become Industrious and Valuable Members of Society
