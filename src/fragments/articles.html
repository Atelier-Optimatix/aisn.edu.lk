Celebrating Independence Day: A Tribute to Freedom and Patriotism

Adventist International School Negombo proudly marked its 76th Independence Day on February 2nd, 2024, with a memorable celebration that epitomized the spirit of liberty and patriotism. Under the esteemed leadership of Headmaster Ronnie Dias Perera and dedicated teachers Sara, Disna, Sheshmi, and Michelli, the school organized a meaningful program that honored the sacrifices of our national heroes and highlighted our collective responsibility as citizens to safeguard and preserve our nation for future generations.

The Independence Day celebration at Adventist International School Negombo was more than just a ceremonial event; it was a solemn reminder of the invaluable gift of freedom bestowed upon us by our forefathers. Through a series of engaging activities and presentations, students were encouraged to reflect on the significance of independence and the enduring values of democracy, equality, and justice.

One of the key objectives of the Independence Day program was to instill a sense of pride and appreciation for our nation's rich heritage and cultural diversity. Through patriotic songs, speeches, and dramatic performances, students were inspired to embrace their identity as Sri Lankan citizens and to honor the sacrifices of those who fought for the freedom we enjoy today.

Furthermore, the program aimed to foster a deeper understanding of civic responsibility and active citizenship among students. Through discussions and interactive sessions, students were encouraged to reflect on their role in shaping the future of our nation and to pledge their commitment to upholding the principles of democracy, tolerance, and social justice.

As we celebrated Independence Day at Adventist International School Negombo, we reaffirmed our dedication to nurturing responsible and compassionate citizens who are empowered to contribute positively to society. By imparting values of integrity, empathy, and respect for diversity, we strive to equip our students with the knowledge and skills they need to become leaders of tomorrow and to build a brighter future for our beloved nation.

In commemorating our nation's independence, we also pay tribute to the countless men and women who have sacrificed and struggled for the cause of freedom. Their courage and resilience serve as a source of inspiration for us all, as we continue to strive for a more just, inclusive, and prosperous society.

As we look to the future with hope and optimism, let us cherish the freedoms we enjoy and recommit ourselves to the noble ideals of democracy, peace, and unity. Together, let us celebrate our nation's heritage and honor the legacy of those who came before us, as we embark on a journey of progress and prosperity for generations to come.

Empowering Tomorrow's Leaders:
Prefect Induction Ceremony 2024

On the auspicious day of January 19th, 2024, Adventist International School – Negombo, witnessed a momentous occasion as the school's new prefects were formally inducted into their roles. Under the guidance and leadership of Headmaster Ronnie Dias, along with the prefect in-charges Teachers Cleesha, Sachinika, Supuni Fernando, and Supuni, the ceremony unfolded as a celebration of leadership, responsibility, and commitment.

The event was graced by the esteemed presence of Mr. Shiron Erinton Perera, the Treasurer of Adventist International School Negombo, who honored the occasion as the chief guest. With pride and enthusiasm, our young leaders were entrusted with their new responsibilities, taking solemn oaths and pledging their dedication to serving their school and peers.

As each prefect stepped forward to accept their role, it was evident that they had been groomed for leadership through seminars and workshops aimed at developing their skills and nurturing their potential. The ceremony not only marked a transition in their academic journey but also symbolized a profound commitment to upholding the values of integrity, service, and excellence.

The prefect induction ceremony was a poignant reminder of the importance of fostering leadership qualities in our youth and providing them with opportunities to grow and thrive. As these young leaders embark on their new roles, they carry with them the hopes and aspirations of their peers, teachers, and school community.

As we reflect on this memorable event, we are reminded of the profound impact that leadership can have in shaping the future. With the support and guidance of their mentors, teachers, and fellow students, we have every confidence that our prefects will rise to the occasion and lead by example, inspiring others to follow in their footsteps.

Adventist International School – Negombo, extends its heartfelt congratulations to the newly inducted prefects and expresses gratitude to all who contributed to the success of the ceremony. Together, let us continue to nurture and empower tomorrow's leaders, guiding them on a path of excellence and service for the betterment of our school and society.

A Melodic Spectacle: Bells & Bows Concert 2022

On the enchanting evening of December 8th, 2022, Adventist International School – Negombo, came alive with the resounding melodies of its highly anticipated concert, themed "Bells & Bows." Under the distinguished patronage of renowned musician Edward Jayakody, the event unfolded as a mesmerizing celebration of talent and harmony, graced by the esteemed presence of our SDA President, Mr. Jerry Patalinghug, and Mrs. Marife Patalinghug.

Led by the visionary coordination of Headmaster Ronnie Dias Perera, along with the dedicated efforts of teachers Nirojini and Gayani, the concert transcended traditional boundaries with a digitalized showcase of performances. From captivating speeches to soul-stirring poems, from melodious songs to captivating dramas, and even sign songs, the program showcased the diverse talents of our students in a new, technologically enhanced format.

The evening was a feast for the senses, with performances delivered in a multitude of languages, reflecting the rich cultural diversity of our school community. Each act unfolded with precision and grace, captivating the audience and leaving a lasting impression of unity and celebration.

As the melodies of "Bells & Bows" reverberated through the halls of Adventist International School – Negombo, it was evident that the concert had not only entertained but also inspired. Through the harmonious collaboration of students, teachers, and esteemed guests, the event served as a testament to the transformative power of music and art in bringing people together.

As we reflect on this memorable evening, we extend our heartfelt appreciation to all who contributed to its success, from performers to organizers, sponsors, and attendees. The "Bells & Bows" Concert 2022 will be remembered as a shining example of the spirit of creativity, collaboration, and community that defines our school.

Until we gather again for our next musical journey, may the melodies of this evening continue to resonate in our hearts and minds, inspiring us to reach greater heights of harmony and unity.
