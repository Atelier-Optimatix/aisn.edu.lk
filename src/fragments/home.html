OUR HISTORY

Nurturing Excellence, Inspiring Futures since 2008

Adventist International School Negombo wanted to open a new Branch  to expand and introduce Adventist education in different regions in Srilanka.  

A new branch of the school was being in several other places before it was permanently relocated in negombo in 2008 under the leadership of Miss. Lalitha Fernando to introduce international education system in Gampaha district. the school was relocated to have access to more urban facilities. During this period the school operated classes up to year 7 (London Curriculum). With many challenges. In 2023 the school is celebrating 15-years journey that witnessed God's enduring power.
