ACADEMICS

We are dedicated to providing a rigorous and comprehensive academic program that nurtures intellectual growth and fosters a love for learning. Our experienced faculty and staff are committed to equipping students with the knowledge, skills, and critical thinking abilities needed to succeed in an ever-evolving world.

Our pre-school program provides a nurturing and stimulating environment for children at different developmental stages.

PRE-KINDERGARTEN
Play, Discover, Learn: Building Foundations for a Bright Future
In our Pre-Kindergarten class, we lay the foundation for learning through interactive play, socialization, and hands-on activities. Our experienced teachers guide young learners as they explore and develop essential skills in language, numeracy, and social-emotional development
LOWER KINDERGARTEN
Growing Curious Minds: Nurturing Learning, Language, and Social Skills
In Lower-Kindergarten, we continue to foster a love for learning while focusing on building language and communication skills, early math concepts, and social skills. Our engaging and age-appropriate activities create a supportive learning environment where children can thrive and grow.
UPPER-KINDERGARTEN
Elevating Young Scholars: Empowering Minds for Academic Excellence
In Upper-Kindergarten, our program prepares children for the next phase of their academic journey. We emphasize language development, critical thinking, problem-solving, and social skills. Our goal is to instill a love for learning and lay a strong foundation for future academic success.

Our primary program caters to students from Year 1 to Year 5, following the Edexcel London Syllabus.

YEAR 1 - YEAR 5
Unleashing Potential, Igniting Excellence: Embracing Global Education
From Year 1 to Year 5, we follow the Edexcel London Syllabus, providing a globally recognized curriculum that nurtures core competencies in English, Mathematics, Science, and other subjects. Through engaging lessons and interactive activities, we foster a love for learning, promote creativity, and develop essential skills for future academic success.
YEAR 6 INTERNATIONAL PRIMARY EXAM
Embracing the Challenge, Excelling with Confidence: Year 6 Primed
In Year 6, students prepare for the International Primary Exam, which serves as a milestone in their primary education journey. Our dedicated teachers provide focused instruction and support, helping students develop a deep understanding of core subjects and ensuring they are well-prepared for the examination.

Our secondary-level education program provides a comprehensive and enriching learning experience. We follow the Edexcel London Syllabus for Year 7 to Year 11.

YEAR 7 TO YEAR 11 (EDEXCEL LONDON SYLLABUS)

Unlocking Potential, Shaping Futures: Empowering Excellence
For students in Year 7 to Year 11, we follow the Edexcel London Syllabus, a globally recognized curriculum that provides a strong foundation in core subjects. Our experienced teachers employ innovative teaching methods and engage students in interactive learning experiences to deepen their understanding of key concepts and develop higher-order thinking skills.

Our curriculum includes the Edexcel London Syllabus for A/S and A/L in Commerce, Science, and Maths.

A/S & A/L - EDEXCEL LONDON SYLLABUS

Embracing Knowledge, Igniting Passions: Empowering Growth
For students pursuing A/S and A/L in Commerce, Science, and Maths, we follow the Edexcel London Syllabus, a globally recognized curriculum that provides in-depth knowledge and skills in these specialized fields. Our dedicated teachers provide comprehensive instruction and guidance to help students excel in their chosen subjects. We emphasize critical thinking, problem-solving, and practical application of concepts to prepare students for higher education and professional careers.

We believe in fostering multilingual abilities, and therefore, French and the mother tongue of students are taught as second languages.

For students following the Edexcel London Syllabus, French, Sinhala, and Tamil are taught as second languages. We recognize the importance of linguistic diversity and provide opportunities for students to develop proficiency in multiple languages.

It is important to note that students from our school who take the Sri Lankan Ordinary Level examinations are considered private candidates by the Department of Examinations of Sri Lanka. We support our students in their preparation for these examinations, providing necessary guidance and resources to help them succeed in their academic pursuits.

We follow the academic calendar based on the London (Edexcel) examination system. Our academic year is divided into three terms: First Term, Second Term, and Third Term.

The First Term, which runs from September to December, marks the beginning of the academic year. During this term, students engage in various learning activities, explore new subjects, and lay the foundation for their educational journey.

The Second Term, which takes place from January to April, provides an opportunity for students to delve deeper into their studies. They consolidate their knowledge, tackle more advanced concepts, and further develop their critical thinking and problem-solving skills.
The Third Term, spanning from May to August, brings the academic year to a close. Students focus on revision, exam preparation, and final assessments. It is a time for consolidation, reflection, and demonstrating their understanding of the subjects they have studied throughout the year.
